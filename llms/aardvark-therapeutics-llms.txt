# Aardvark Therapeutics

> Aardvark Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: AARD) is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company
> developing novel small-molecule therapies designed to inhibit hunger and treat metabolic
> disease. Its lead candidate, ARD-101, is an oral, gut-restricted agonist of bitter taste
> receptors (TAS2Rs) on the luminal side of the intestine that triggers the release of satiety
